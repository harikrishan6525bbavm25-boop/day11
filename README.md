# Day 11 — Lab 11: Canva Magic Studio

## Aviation Mini-Campaign and Brand Consistency

### 📌 Overview

This lab focused on using **Canva Magic Studio and AI-assisted design features** to create a professional aviation mini-campaign while maintaining consistent branding across multiple communication formats.

The campaign was developed for a fictional organisation, **AeroVista Airports**, under the campaign **“Travel Smart at the Airport.”** The main objective was to encourage passengers to prepare before travelling and verify travel information through official airline and airport channels.

The activity included campaign planning, brand-guide creation, AI image generation, image expansion and editing, social-media design, passenger-awareness poster creation, presentation visuals, resizing, brand-consistency checking, peer review, and responsible AI evaluation.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this lab were to:

* Create a structured aviation communication campaign.
* Develop and apply a consistent brand guide.
* Use Canva Magic Media to generate an aviation-related visual.
* Evaluate AI-generated images for accuracy and professionalism.
* Use Magic Expand to adapt visuals to different formats.
* Use Magic Edit to correct or improve AI-generated content.
* Create social-media, poster, and presentation formats.
* Maintain consistent colours, fonts, visual style, and tone.
* Identify AI-generated inaccuracies and misleading information.
* Apply responsible AI and privacy principles.
* Conduct peer review and revise the final campaign.

## ✈️ Campaign Brief

### Organisation

**AeroVista Airports** *(fictional organisation)*

### Campaign

**Travel Smart at the Airport**

### Objective

Encourage domestic passengers to prepare before travelling and rely on official airline and airport channels for accurate travel information.

### Target Audience

* Domestic airline passengers
* Students
* Young travellers
* First-time flyers
* Occasional flyers

### Communication Channels

* Social media
* Airport passenger-awareness poster
* Business presentation

### Primary Message

> **Check official travel information before arriving at the airport.**

### Supporting Messages

1. Check flight status using official airline channels.
2. Keep travel documents accessible.
3. Follow verified baggage and security instructions.

## 🤖 Canva Magic Media

### AI Image Prompt

The Magic Media prompt was designed to generate a realistic aviation environment showing passengers preparing for travel and checking official flight information.

Key prompt requirements included:

* Diverse adult passengers
* Modern airport terminal
* Digital flight-information display
* Travel documents
* Blue-and-white colour palette
* Bright natural lighting
* Realistic airport environment
* Photorealistic aviation-documentary style
* Natural human poses
* No airline logos
* No airport names
* No readable personal information
* No invented baggage or security information
* No promotional text

Four images were generated, and **Image 2** was selected because it provided realistic passengers, a clean airport environment, natural lighting, and suitable space for campaign text.

## 🖼️ Magic Expand

The original image was expanded from a **square format to a 16:9 landscape format** for presentation use.

The expanded image provided additional horizontal space, but the newly generated background areas required inspection because AI expansion can introduce unexpected objects or architectural details.

### Review Areas

* Composition
* Passenger appearance
* Airport background
* Lighting
* Unwanted elements

Minor background details were identified for possible cropping or reframing.

---

## ✏️ Magic Edit

Magic Edit was used to improve an unclear digital airport display.

### Editing Instruction

> Replace the unclear display content with a clean, neutral airport information screen without readable flight details.

The edited display became visually cleaner and less likely to imply false operational information. A final visual inspection was still required.

## 🔎 Brand and Accuracy Audit

The campaign was reviewed for:

* Brand consistency
* Accuracy
* Professionalism
* Privacy
* Responsible AI use

### Key Findings

* Minor spacing differences existed between formats.
* AI-generated display text could be unclear.
* Some background details required inspection.
* Passenger information remained non-readable.
* AI-generated artwork could potentially be mistaken for a real airport photograph.

Corrections included manual spacing adjustments, neutral/blank display content, cropping distracting details, continued privacy checks, and appropriate treatment of the visual as campaign artwork/AI-assisted content where required.

---

## 📐 Resize and Adapt

The campaign was adapted into three formats:

1. **Square social-media post**
2. **Portrait passenger-awareness poster**
3. **16:9 presentation visual**

During resizing, the following issues were checked and corrected:

* Cut-off text
* Image distortion
* Poor spacing
* Small font sizes
* Alignment
* Footer visibility
* Visual hierarchy

The presentation version was simplified to reduce excessive text and improve readability.

# 🔐 My Aviation Mini-Campaign — Passenger Cyber-Safety

A second mini-campaign was developed around **Passenger Cyber-Safety**.

### Campaign

**Click Smart Before You Fly**

### Objective

Encourage passengers to verify digital travel information and avoid relying on suspicious or unverified online messages.

### Target Audience

Domestic airline passengers, especially students and young travellers who frequently use mobile devices.

### Key Messages

* Use official airline and airport websites or apps.
* Be cautious with unexpected links claiming to provide flight updates.
* Do not share sensitive personal or travel information with unknown sources.
* Verify important information through authorised channels.

### Restrictions

The campaign avoided:

* Fake airline messages
* Real passenger information
* Claims about specific cyber incidents
* Fabricated statistics
* Real airline logos
* Unverified claims about specific websites or messages.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

Through this lab, I learned:

1. A **brand guide** helps maintain consistent colours, fonts, visual style, and communication tone.
2. Canva Magic Media can provide a useful starting point for aviation campaign visuals.
3. AI-generated images require human review for accuracy and realism.
4. Magic Expand can introduce unexpected background objects and architectural details.
5. Magic Edit can be used to correct unclear or potentially misleading visual elements.
6. AI-generated text should always be checked because it may contain spelling errors, distorted characters, or inaccurate information.
7. Aviation information should be verified through official airline and airport sources.
8. Brand consistency can be maintained across social posts, posters, and presentations using the same visual system.
9. Resizing requires manual adjustment of spacing, text, alignment, and hierarchy.
10. Peer review helps identify design problems that may not be immediately visible to the original creator.
11. AI-assisted designs should be reviewed for accuracy, privacy, professionalism, branding, and responsible use.
